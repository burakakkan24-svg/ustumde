Üstümde · Help and contact

If you run into a problem with Üstümde, or have a suggestion or question, write to ustumde.app@gmail.com. Including your phone model and iOS version helps me help you faster.

Where is my data kept?

Your wardrobe, photos and outfits are stored only on your phone. There is no account or server, so we cannot see your data or restore it for you.

I am changing phones. What happens to my wardrobe?

On the old phone, go to Profile > Backup > "Create backup" and save the file to iCloud Drive or Files. On the new phone, install Üstümde and choose that file with Profile > Backup > "Restore".

The background was not removed well

  • Lay the item on a flat, plain surface (a bedspread, a door, a wall).
  • Shoot in good light, close enough to fill the screen.
  • If the item and the surface are the same colour, try a different surface.

An item does not sit right on the mannequin

In Try on, tap "Auto-align". If it is still off, tap the item and use the slider next to it to make it bigger or smaller, or move it with "Adjust".

Voice commands, weather or notifications do not work

Turn on the permission in your phone’s Settings > Üstümde: Microphone and Speech Recognition for voice commands, Location for the weather, Notifications for reminders.

How do I delete all my data?

"Delete all my data" at the bottom of Profile permanently deletes your wardrobe, photos, outfits and settings. Deleting the app from your phone also deletes all of its data.
