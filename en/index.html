Privacy Policy

Üstümde · Last updated: October 26, 2026

Üstümde does not collect personal data about you. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your wardrobe, photos, outfits and settings are stored only on your phone; we cannot see them because we have no server.

What stays on your phone

The following is kept only on your device, in the app's own folder:

  • Items in your wardrobe: name, type, colour, season, use and dressiness
  • Item photos and their background-removed versions
  • Saved outfits, favourites and "Recently deleted" (for 30 days)
  • The name you enter in Profile, settings such as theme and reminder time, your recent searches
  • In-app notifications
  • The last weather reading (to show it when you are offline; your location itself is not stored)

Permissions

All are optional. Declining a permission only turns off the related feature; the rest of the app keeps working.

  • Camera: to take item photos
  • Photos: to pick item photos from your library
  • Location: to show the weather on the home screen, only while the app is open
  • Microphone and speech recognition: for voice commands, only when you tap the microphone button
  • Notifications: for daily outfit and backup reminders (scheduled on your phone)

Information that leaves your phone: weather

To show the weather, your location, rounded to about 1 km, is sent to the Open-Meteo weather service (open-meteo.com). The response is used only to show the current weather and is not stored by us. No account or identity information is sent to Open-Meteo; as with any internet request, Open-Meteo can see the connection's IP address. Open-Meteo's own terms apply.

The name of your district is looked up with your phone's operating-system location service (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android).

If you do not grant location permission, the weather is not shown; nothing else is affected.

Voice commands

If you tap the microphone button when asking for an outfit, what you say is turned into text by your phone's own speech recognition. If your phone supports it, this happens entirely on your phone and no audio leaves it.

If your phone cannot recognise speech on the device, the recording is sent to your phone's operating-system provider (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android) to be turned into text; their terms apply. The recording is not stored by the app; only the resulting sentence is used for the outfit suggestion.

If you do not use voice commands, the microphone is never turned on.

Background removal and colour detection

The AI model that removes photo backgrounds and detects clothing colours is built into the app and runs entirely on your phone. Your photos are never uploaded.

Backups

You create the backup file and decide where to save it (e.g. iCloud Drive, Files, email). The privacy terms of the service you choose apply. The backup file is not encrypted; keep it somewhere you trust.

If your phone's own backup (e.g. iCloud device backup) is on, the app's data may be included in it.

Deleting your data

  • You can delete items and outfits inside the app. Deleted items stay in "Recently deleted" for 30 days and are then permanently deleted; you can also delete them right away.
  • Deleting the app from your phone deletes all of its data from the phone.

Children

Üstümde does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page and the date above is updated.
