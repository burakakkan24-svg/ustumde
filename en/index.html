Privacy Policy

Üstümde · Last updated: November 6, 2026

Üstümde does not collect personal data about you. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your wardrobe, photos, outfits and settings are stored only on your phone; we cannot see them because we have no server.

What stays on your phone

The following is kept only on your device, in the app's own folder:

  • Items in your wardrobe: name, type, colour, season, use and dressiness
  • Item photos and their background-removed versions
  • Saved outfits, favourites and "Recently deleted" (for 30 days)
  • The name you enter in Profile, settings such as theme and reminder time, your recent searches
  • In-app notifications
  • The last weather reading (to show it when you are offline; your location itself is not stored)

Permissions

All are optional. Declining a permission only turns off the related feature; the rest of the app keeps working.

  • Camera: to take item photos
  • Photos: to pick item photos from your library
  • Location: to show the weather on the home screen, only while the app is open
  • Microphone and speech recognition: for voice commands, only when you tap the microphone button
  • Notifications: for daily outfit and backup reminders (scheduled on your phone)

Information that leaves your phone: weather

To show the weather, your location, rounded to about 1 km, is sent to the Open-Meteo weather service (open-meteo.com). The response is used only to show the current weather and is not stored by us. No account or identity information is sent to Open-Meteo; as with any internet request, Open-Meteo can see the connection's IP address. Open-Meteo's own terms apply.

The name of your district is looked up with your phone's operating-system location service (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android).

If you do not grant location permission, the weather is not shown; nothing else is affected.

Voice commands

If you tap the microphone button when asking for an outfit, what you say is turned into text by your phone's own speech recognition. If your phone supports it, this happens entirely on your phone and no audio leaves it.

If your phone cannot recognise this language on the device, the app asks for your permission before any audio is sent. If you allow it, the recording is sent to your phone's operating-system provider (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android) only to be turned into text; their terms apply. If you do not, no audio leaves your phone and you can type instead. You can withdraw your permission at any time in Profile > Voice commands.

The recording is not stored by the app; only the resulting sentence is used for the outfit suggestion.

If you do not use voice commands, the microphone is never turned on.

Background removal and colour detection

The AI model that removes photo backgrounds and detects clothing colours is built into the app and runs entirely on your phone. Your photos are never uploaded.

Backups

You create the backup file and decide where to save it (e.g. iCloud Drive, Files, email). The privacy terms of the service you choose apply. The backup file is not encrypted; keep it somewhere you trust.

If your phone's own backup (e.g. iCloud device backup) is on, the app's data may be included in it.

Deleting your data

  • You can delete items and outfits inside the app. Deleted items stay in "Recently deleted" for 30 days and are then permanently deleted; you can also delete them right away.
  • Profile > "Delete all my data" permanently deletes your items, photos, outfits and settings in one go.
  • Deleting the app from your phone deletes all of its data from the phone.

Turkish data protection law (KVKK) notice

This section informs you under Article 10 of Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

Data controller: Burak Akkan, developer of the Üstümde app. Contact: ustumde.app@gmail.com

Data processed and why: What you enter in the app (items, photos, outfits, your name and settings) is processed only on your phone so that the app works; it is not sent to the data controller or anywhere else. The only personal data that leaves your phone is your approximate location and your connection's IP address, to show the weather, and, when you use voice commands, your phone cannot recognise speech on the device and you have given permission, your voice recording (only to turn it into text).

How it is collected and legal basis: Data is collected electronically from what you enter and the permissions you grant (location, microphone) while using the app. The weather is needed to provide the feature you choose to use (KVKK Article 5/2-c); you can stop it at any time by revoking location permission in your phone's settings. Sending a voice recording off the phone relies only on the explicit consent you give in the app before anything is sent (KVKK Articles 5/1 and 9); you can withdraw it at any time in Profile > Voice commands.

Transfers: Approximate location and IP address go to the Open-Meteo weather service; voice recordings, only with your explicit consent, go to your phone's operating-system provider (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android). Their servers are outside Turkey, so this counts as a transfer abroad. It happens only when you use the feature and only as much as the feature needs.

Retention: Data stays on your phone until you delete it. The data controller does not store any of your personal data.

Under KVKK Article 11 you have the right to:

  • Learn whether your personal data is processed and, if so, request information about it
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly
  • Know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred
  • Ask for it to be corrected if it is incomplete or wrong
  • Ask for it to be deleted or destroyed under KVKK Article 7
  • Ask for corrections and deletions to be notified to the third parties it was transferred to
  • Object to a result against you that arises solely from automated analysis
  • Claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing
  • Requests: Write to ustumde.app@gmail.com; your request is answered free of charge within 30 days at the latest. Because your data is on your phone, you can also do things like deletion yourself right away in the app (Profile > "Delete all my data").

Children

Üstümde does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.
