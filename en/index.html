What stays on your phone

The following is kept only on your device, in the app's own folder:

  • Items in your wardrobe: name, type, colour, season, use and dressiness
  • Item photos and their background-removed versions
  • Saved outfits, favourites and "Recently deleted" (for 30 days)
  • The name you enter in Profile, settings such as theme and reminder time, your recent searches
  • In-app notifications

Permissions

All are optional. Declining a permission only turns off the related feature; the rest of the app keeps working.

  • Camera: to take item photos
  • Photos: to pick item photos from your library
  • Microphone and speech recognition: for voice commands, only when you tap the microphone button
  • Notifications: for daily outfit and backup reminders (scheduled on your phone)

Voice commands

If you tap the microphone button when asking for an outfit, what you say is turned into text by your phone's own speech recognition. If your phone supports it, this happens entirely on your phone and no audio leaves it.

If your phone cannot recognise this language on the device, the app asks for your explicit consent before any audio is sent. If you consent, the recording is sent to your phone's operating-system provider (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android) only to be turned into text; that provider's terms apply. If you do not consent, no audio leaves your phone and you can type instead. You can withdraw your consent at any time in Profile > Voice commands.

The recording is not stored by the app; only the resulting sentence is used for the outfit suggestion.

If you do not use voice commands, the microphone is never turned on.

Background removal and colour detection

The AI model that removes photo backgrounds and detects clothing colours is built into the app and runs entirely on your phone. Your photos are never uploaded.

Backups

You create the backup file and decide where to save it (e.g. iCloud Drive, Files, email). The privacy terms of the service you choose apply. The backup file is not encrypted; keep it somewhere you trust.

If your phone's own backup (e.g. iCloud device backup) is on, the app's data may be included in it.

Deleting your data

  • You can delete your items and outfits inside the app. Deleted items stay in "Recently deleted" for 30 days and are then permanently deleted; you can also delete them right away.
  • Profile > "Delete all my data" permanently deletes your items, photos, outfits and settings in one go.
  • Deleting the app from your phone deletes all of its data from the phone.

Personal Data Protection (KVKK) Notice

This notice is provided under Article 10 of Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") and the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles for Fulfilling the Obligation to Inform.

1. Data controller

  • Data controller: Burak Akkan (developer of the Üstümde app)
  • Email: ustumde.app@gmail.com

2. Personal data processed and purposes

  • What you enter in the app (items, photos, outfits, your name and settings): Processed only on your phone so that the app works; it is not sent to the data controller or anywhere else.
  • Voice recording: Processed only to turn a voice command into text, and only when your phone cannot recognise the language on the device and you have given your explicit consent.
  • Your email address and message: If you email us yourself, processed only so that we can reply to you.

3. How data is collected and legal basis

Data is collected electronically from what you enter and the permissions you grant (microphone) while using the app.

  • Voice recording: Sending it off the phone relies only on the explicit consent you give separately in the app before anything is sent (KVKK Articles 5/1 and 9). You can withdraw your consent at any time in Profile > Voice commands; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.
  • Emails: Processed to answer your request, within the legitimate interest of the data controller (KVKK Article 5/2-f), in a way that does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms.

4. Transfers of personal data

Your voice recording is passed, only with your explicit consent, to your phone's operating-system provider (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android) to be turned into text. Because this service's servers are located outside Turkey, this is a transfer abroad and is made in line with the conditions of KVKK Article 9. A transfer happens only when you use the feature and only as much as the feature needs.

Emails you send us are stored abroad on the servers of the email service we use (Gmail, Google).

Apart from this, your personal data is not shared with third parties.

5. Retention

Data in the app stays on your phone until you delete it; the data controller does not access or store it. Emails you send us are kept for at most 1 year after your request is resolved and are then deleted or destroyed. If you want them deleted sooner, just write to us.

6. Your rights under KVKK Article 11

By applying to the data controller, you can:

  • Learn whether your personal data is processed
  • Request information about it if it is processed
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used for that purpose
  • Know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred
  • Ask for it to be corrected if it is incomplete or wrong
  • Ask for it to be deleted or destroyed under KVKK Article 7
  • Ask for corrections and deletions to be notified to the third parties it was transferred to
  • Object to a result against you that arises solely from analysis by automated systems
  • Claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing

7. How to apply

You can send your request, with information that identifies you and an explanation of the right you want to use, in these ways:

  • Signed with a secure electronic signature, to ustumde.app@gmail.com,
  • From the email address you previously wrote to us from, to ustumde.app@gmail.com.

Requests are resolved within 30 days at the latest, depending on their nature. If the process involves an additional cost, a fee may be charged according to the tariff set by the Personal Data Protection Board.

Because your data is on your phone, you can also do things like deletion yourself right away in the app (Profile > "Delete all my data").

8. Right to complain

If you find the answer to your request insufficient, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board within 30 days of learning the answer and in any case within 60 days of the date of your request.

Children

Üstümde is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a minor, you must get the consent of your parent or legal guardian before using the app.
